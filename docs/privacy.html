Privacy Policy

Mr File Fixit (Android app com.mrfilefixit.app)
Last updated: 31 October 2026

Mr File Fixit ("the app") is a free, offline tool for fixing photos, signatures, PDFs, scans and resumes. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your files stay on your device.

What the app accesses

Only the files, photos and scans you choose. The app has no storage permission; Android's file and photo pickers hand it the specific files you select. The app does not request your location, contacts, microphone or account information.

Where your files are processed

On your device only. The app does not upload your files, store them on a server, or share them with anyone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not request the Internet permission and contains no network code of its own.

Data stored on your device

  • Temporary files. Working copies are kept in the app's private cache while a tool runs and are deleted afterwards. Anything left over is removed the next time the app starts.
  • Your results. Output files are saved only where you choose, or shared only with the app you pick in Android's share sheet.
  • Resume drafts and settings. Resume details you type and your app preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere, are excluded from cloud backup, and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • PDF passwords you enter to protect or unlock a PDF are used once and never stored.

Metadata removal

By default, image exports strip EXIF data such as GPS location and camera details, and PDF compression removes document metadata, so your outputs do not reveal where a photo was taken.

Third-party components

The document scanner and text recognition (OCR) use Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. Your images and documents are not sent to Google. Google Play services, which provides ML Kit, may download scanner components and may send anonymous usage and performance statistics to Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Personal data we collect

None. We do not collect, sell or share personal or sensitive user data. Because nothing leaves your device, there is no data on our side to access, correct or delete.

Exam specifications

Exam photo and signature presets are provided for convenience only. They are not official and the app is not affiliated with any exam body. Always check the official notification.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
