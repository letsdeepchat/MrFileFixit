Mr File Fixit privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Mr File Fixit ("the app") is a free offline file tool. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What the app accesses

Only the files, photos and scans you pick. The app has no storage permission; Android hands it the files you choose.

Where it is processed

On your device only. The app does not upload, store on a server or share your files, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking of its own.

No internet access

The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything, and neither can any component inside it.

Temporary files

Working copies live in the app's private cache and are deleted when you leave a tool; anything left behind is removed the next time the app starts. Results are saved only where you choose.

Resume and biodata drafts

What you type into a resume or biodata, and the photos you add to it, are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone so you can come back to them. They are not backed up, and they are removed when you delete the draft or uninstall the app.

Metadata

Image exports strip location, camera details and other EXIF data, and PDF compression removes document metadata, so outputs do not reveal where a photo was taken.

Third-party components

Text recognition and Remove background use Google ML Kit models that are built into the app and run on the device. The document scanner is shown by Google Play services, which processes the scan on your device and hands the pages back to the app (Google privacy policy).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
